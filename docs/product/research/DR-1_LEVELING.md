## 1. Anchor

Ayush is a **Google L5 PM operating near L6**. Target: roles **comparable to his current scope** (D-008), i.e. L5–L6-equivalent IC roles, with no direct reports by default (CR-6).

## 2. Equivalency map (v1)

| Company / type | ≈ Google L5 | ≈ Google L6 | Confidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google | PM / Senior PM (L5) | Senior PM / Group PM (IC) (L6) | High | Title vs. level varies by org |
| Amazon | Sr. PM / Sr. PM-T (L6) | Principal PM (L7) | Medium | Matches Ayush's own view (L5 ≈ Amazon L6/L7). Amazon levels are wide |
| Meta | Product Manager (IC5) | Senior PM (IC6) | Medium | |
| Microsoft | Senior PM (63–64) | Principal PM (65–66) | Medium | |

| Canadian banks and insurers (RBC, TD, BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, Manulife, Sun Life) | Senior Manager / Manager, Product | Director, Product (IC-leaning), Senior Director | Low | Bank titles are inflated vs. tech, and "Director" often **includes reports**, so check CR-6 |
| Canadian scale-ups (Wealthsimple, Cohere, Clio, Faire, 1Password, Ada…) | Senior PM | Staff / Principal PM, Group PM (IC) | Low–Med | Calibrate from posted ranges (Ontario pay transparency) |
| Startups (Seed–Series B) | "Head of Product," Lead PM | Head of / VP Product (often with reports) | Low | Scope varies widely. Use headcount, stage and scope language |

## 3. Level signals to extract from a JD (for B6)

- Title keywords: Senior / Staff / Principal / Group / Lead / Head
- Years of experience asked for (L5 ≈ 5–8+ years of PM-relevant experience. L6 ≈ 8–12+)
- Scope language: "set strategy for X," "multi-team," "org-level," "exec stakeholders," "ambiguity"
- Reports: "manage a team of PMs" (sets CR-6)
- Pay range, where posted. Ontario requires ranges up to $200K (DR-4), which is a strong level proxy
